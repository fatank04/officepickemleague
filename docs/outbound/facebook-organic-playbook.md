# Facebook-Organic Playbook — the founder-time social channel (new 2026-07-19)

Replaces the weak LinkedIn layer. **Organic Facebook only — Groups + friend requests + DMs.
NOT Facebook ads** (paid geo-social is still cut — see gtm-plan.md; the ads learning-phase math
doesn't work at our budget). This is **$0, founder-time, no ad account**. Method adapted from
Zane Cole's "AI Client Method" (youtu.be/wZ73VhKZwZw) — his channel of choice for reaching local
owner-operated SMBs.

## Why it fits OPL better than LinkedIn

- **Audience is a near-exact overlap.** His targets — HVAC, auto shops, plumbers, electricians,
  construction, property management *owners* — are precisely our "owner-is-the-buyer" ICP
  (companies ≤150; see apollo-icp-spec.md). The research already found blue-collar owners
  **under-index on LinkedIn**; this method's whole thesis is *they're on Facebook instead*, with
  their guard down, and nobody's prospecting them there.
- **Our offer is lower-friction than his.** He sells $2k–15k AI retainers, so he *needs* the
  discovery-call motion. We're **free to start, "I set it up myself."** So we **close in the DM**
  (get headcount → build the league same-day → send the link) — usually no call needed. The
  free-to-start removes the exact friction his method engineers around.
- Free, ~30 min/day (fits the 5–10 hrs/wk founder budget), additive to kits + calls + email.

## Guardrails (read first)

- **Real identity, always.** Ankur's real name, real face photo, real posts. Faceless/fake
  accounts get ignored and banned. Honest group-question answers. This is also a strength — a
  face + name builds trust cold email can't.
- **Warm up before DMing** (1–2 weeks). A brand-new/dead profile that starts mass-friending gets
  restricted. Look like a real person: comment, post, engage first.
- **Stay under Facebook's rails.** 10–15 friend requests/day to start; it rises as mutual-friend
  count grows. If FB says "slow down," slow down. **No automation ever** (ban risk; the founder
  account is irreplaceable — same rule as the old LinkedIn layer).
- **Never pitch in the DM.** The disclaimer *is* the sales alarm. Ask questions until the fit is
  clear, then make the (free) offer.

## Step 1 — Profile that passes the 3-second check

Owners check your profile the second you friend/message them. Top to bottom:
- **Photo:** real, friendly headshot. **Cover:** personal (you/family), not a company graphic —
  a graphic reads as "here to sell" and can block group entry.
- **Bio, one line, who you help:** e.g. *"I help local employers run a no-money office football
  league — team engagement, zero gambling."* Filters for the buyer, filters out the rest.
- **Details:** city (Pittsburgh), the site (officepickemleague.com) as the landing link. You do
  NOT need many friends — 30–40 owner connections was enough for his first client.

## Step 2 — Seed 3 post types (rotate; 1–2/week)

Not social-media marketing — just enough activity to prove you're real. No fabricated
customers/testimonials (see Rules).
1. **Human:** real life, family, weekend. Proves you're a person, not a lead-gen bot.
2. **Authority:** an engagement insight in the owner's language, no CTA. e.g. *"The shops where
   the floor and the front office actually talk on Monday? Half the time it's because someone
   started a football pool. Gallup says teams that gel run 63% fewer safety incidents."*
3. **Proof (soft):** the honest proof we have — Gallup Q12 numbers (match CANONICAL_FACTS), the
   live demo league, the "18 weeks for less than one team lunch" math. The walkthrough video.

## Step 3 — Find & join the groups (the watering hole)

Search `[niche] owners` → **Groups** filter. Join 5–10 (one great active group can carry it —
he runs off a single group). Target **private, owner-focused, ≥1,000 members, active (several
posts/day).**

**OPL Pittsburgh blue-collar target groups (search strings):**
- Blue-collar owner niches (his exact overlap): `auto shop owners`, `HVAC business owners`,
  `plumbing business owners`, `electrical contractor owners`, `landscaping business owners`,
  `construction business owners`, `trucking / fleet owners`, `machine shop / manufacturing owners`,
  `property management owners`.
- Local: `Pittsburgh small business owners`, `Pittsburgh business network`,
  `Western PA business owners`, `Butler/Washington/Beaver County business`.
- Buyer-role: `HR professionals [Pittsburgh]`, `office managers group`, `SHRM Pittsburgh`.
- Product-gated (paid but high-value, his trick): owner communities behind a tool subscription
  (e.g. a shop-management software's owners group) — a small monthly fee buys a private pool of
  qualified, spend-willing owners. Evaluate case-by-case.
- Membership answers (honest): *"I run a no-money football pick'em league for workplaces,"* or
  *"I work with local employers on team engagement,"* + "yes, I'll follow the rules." Never lie;
  they can see from your profile.

## Step 4 — Warm up (week 1 inside the groups)

Not selling — becoming a familiar name. Daily: **comment on 2–3 posts** (useful/human), **send
10–15 friend requests** to owners you've interacted with or who posted recently (a request from
"the guy who answered my question yesterday" gets accepted). Keep posting to your feed. After a
week: 50–100 owner connections who've seen your name → real conversations can start. It snowballs
as mutual-friends rise (FB starts recommending owners to you).

## Step 5 — The 4-stage DM (adapted for OPL's free offer)

Zero pitch in stages 1–3. One or two questions per message. Talk like one person to another.

- **Stage 1 — light open** (get a reply, zero business): *"Hey [name], thanks for the add — how's
  business treating the shop this fall?"* / *"Saw your post in [group] — how long you been running
  the place?"*
- **Stage 2 — their world** (ask about the crew, not their marketing): *"How big's the crew these
  days?"* · *"One location or split shifts?"* · *"Y'all do anything for the team during football
  season?"*
- **Stage 3 — one insight** (be useful, earn the offer): reflect something back. *"Funny — the
  crews that run a free office pool are the ones where the floor and the office actually talk on
  Mondays. Costs nothing, whole building's in it."* (Soft engagement insight, NOT a hard-dollar
  diagnosis — our value is culture/morale, not a bleeding-neck ROI, so lead with fun + free +
  seasonal, not a loss calculation.)
- **Stage 4 — bridge to the FREE-league close** (not a sales call): *"I actually run a no-money
  NFL pick'em league for crews like yours — whole team plays by text or on paper, two minutes a
  week, nobody puts in a dime, and it's free to start. Want me to set one up for [company] before
  kickoff? Takes me five minutes; you just forward one link."* → **close in the DM**: get headcount,
  build the league same-day, send the invite link + commissioner cheat sheet. Offer a 15-min
  walkthrough ONLY if they hesitate. (This is where OPL diverges hard from the source method:
  free product = no discovery/sales call, close conversationally — same as the email reply
  playbook in sequence-copy.md.)

## Step 6 — Daily 30 minutes (5 days/week, ~2.5 hrs/wk)

- 5 min — reply to every open conversation (in-motion convos come first)
- 10 min — comment on 2–3 group posts (stay familiar; compounds)
- 5 min — send 10–15 friend requests to active owners
- 10 min — send openers to everyone who accepted since yesterday
- + 1–2 feed posts/week (Step 2)

## The math (adapted — expect HIGHER convo→win than his)

His funnel: ~70 requests/wk → ~35 accepts → 10–25 convos → 2–4 booked calls → close 1 every
couple weeks (at $2k–15k). **Ours converts better at the bottom** because the ask is a *free*
league, not a paid retainer — a positive conversation ≈ an activation, no call gate. Rough
estimate once warmed: **~2–5 leagues/month.** Slow to start (1–2 wk warmup), so **modest before
Sept 9, compounds after** — treat as a season-long + evergreen channel ("leagues start any week;
scoring begins where you join").
