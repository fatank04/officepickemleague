call earns the conversation, the conversation becomes a league.** A kit that never gets a
follow-up call is half a kit — budget assumes founder calls on all 50.

  chase within 2–3 days. Log tracking in refinement col O.

## Call playbook (free — this is founder time, ~3–4 hrs/wk in the windows)
- **Windows:** 8–9am ET first (owners at the desk pre-shift), then 4–5pm. Wed/Thu best.
  SMB owners answer their own phones — expect 15–25% connects, triple the B2B norm.
- **Cadence:** first attempt 2–3 days after tracked delivery; 3–4 attempts total, spaced
  ~3 days; log every attempt in refinement col P.
- **Opener:** "Morning {{firstName}} — Ankur. I'm the one who mailed you the football.
  Did it land on your desk?" *(pause — this line does all the work)*
- **The pitch (15 seconds):** "It wasn't a stunt — I want to run your office football pool
  this season. No money in it, no app, whole crew plays two minutes a week — text, web,
  or those paper sheets in the box. Free to start, and I do the setup myself.
  Want me to have {{company}}'s league live before kickoff?"
- **Yes →** get roster size + how the crew communicates; build the league SAME DAY; send the
  invite link + commissioner cheat sheet; offer to print their real Week-1 sheets.
- **Gatekeeper →** "Who runs morale over there — the office manager? The football's probably
  on their desk." Get a name, address the next attempt to them.
- **Objections:** gambling → "There's no money anywhere in it — no buy-ins, no pots. Prizes
  come from you, not the crew. That's the whole point." · time → "Two minutes a week; even the
  firm behind the 'fantasy football costs billions' headline says office leagues build
  camaraderie." · not now → "Leagues can start any week — scoring starts where you join.
  I'll check back before Week 4."
- **Voicemail (≤15 sec, attempt 2 only):** "Ankur — the football guy. Free office football
  pool, I do all the work, kickoff's Sept 9. Email's in your inbox, name's on the box."
- **Dialer:** Quo (ex-OpenPhone) ~$15/mo or Google Voice — one local-friendly number, ~$35
  total for the campaign. Do NOT call from the personal cell (caller-ID hygiene + a number
  that can be answered/texted later).

## Facebook second-touch (free, manual — kit recipients)
When you've got a kit owner's name, send a Facebook friend request as a warm second touch — no
pitch in the request ("I'm the one who mailed the football" is the note). This dovetails with
the standalone **Facebook-organic channel** (groups + DMs to the wider metro) — see
facebook-organic-playbook.md for the full method, the 4-stage DM, and the daily routine. NEVER
automate — the founder account is irreplaceable.
