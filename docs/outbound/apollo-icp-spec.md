# Apollo ICP Filter Spec — Pittsburgh Wave 1

Paste-ready saved-search definitions for Apollo (Search → People / Companies). Goal: build the
outbound universe *around* the 25 kit accounts — every qualified Pittsburgh account the kits
won't reach. Export → refinement schema (`refinement-schema.md`) → Instantly.

## Account (company) filters

| Filter | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Location (company HQ) | Pittsburgh, PA metro (include Allegheny, Westmoreland, Washington, Butler, Beaver counties) | Wave-1 geography; 412 pilot |
| Employee headcount | 25–1,000 | Below 25 the flat tier doesn't pencil for buyers; above 1,000 = Enterprise motion, not cold email |
| Industry (include) | Manufacturing · Logistics & Supply Chain · Trucking/Freight · Construction · Wholesale/Distribution · Facilities & Field Services · Automotive Service · Food Production/Beverage | Deskless, shift-based, football-town blue-collar — the brand's home turf |
| Industry (exclude) | Gambling/casinos · staffing agencies · churches/nonprofits w/ <10 staff | Brand-risk or no-budget |
| Keywords (nice-to-have) | "family owned", "three shifts", "union", "plant", "yard", "dispatch" | Signals shift culture; boosts score in refinement |

## Contact (person) filters — priority order

1. **Owner / President / GM** (companies 25–150) — at this size the owner IS the buyer.
2. **HR Manager / HR Director / People Ops** (150–1,000) — owns engagement budget
   ($100–500/employee/yr team-building spend is their line item).
3. **Office Manager / Operations Manager** — the likely *commissioner*; champion motion.

Per account: pull max 2 contacts (1 buyer + 1 champion). More = duplicate-outreach risk.

## Exports & hygiene

- Export with: name, title, company, headcount, industry, LinkedIn URL, company domain, city.
- Apollo emails are ~70–85% accurate — do NOT send to raw exports. Everything passes the
  verification step in the refinement schema first.
- Volume target: 300–500 accounts / 500–800 contacts for wave 1. That's ~2 months of sending
  at safe volumes (see metrics-sheet.md) — don't over-export; credits don't refund.
