# Apollo ICP Filter Spec — Pittsburgh Wave 1 (v2)

Paste-ready saved-search definitions for Apollo (Search → People / Companies). v2 job is
double: (1) hand-pick the **50 kit accounts** (Rings 1–2), (2) build the **email universe**
(Ring 3) — every other qualified Pittsburgh account. Export → refinement schema
(`refinement-schema.md`) → Instantly. Ring definitions: gtm-plan.md.

## STEP 0 — list-depth check (do this before sizing anything)

Run the account filters below and record the raw count. Ring 3's plan assumes
**~1,000–1,500 contactable people**. If the qualified-account count × 2 contacts lands under
~800, widen in this order (never widen title quality): 1) add Healthcare clinics/home-care +
Property management/janitorial, 2) stretch headcount floor to 20, 3) add outer-metro counties
(Armstrong, Fayette, Lawrence). Log what you widened — it's a finding, not a footnote.

## Account (company) filters

| Filter | Value | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Location (company HQ) | Pittsburgh, PA metro (Allegheny, Westmoreland, Washington, Butler, Beaver counties) | Wave-1 geography; 412 pilot |
| Employee headcount | 25–1,000 | Below 25 the flat tier doesn't pencil; above 1,000 = Enterprise motion, not cold outreach |
| Industry (include) | Manufacturing · Logistics & Supply Chain · Trucking/Freight · Construction · Wholesale/Distribution · Facilities & Field Services · Automotive Service · Food Production/Beverage | Deskless, shift-based, football-town blue-collar — the brand's home turf |
| Industry (exclude) | Gambling/casinos · staffing agencies · churches/nonprofits w/ <10 staff | Brand-risk or no-budget |
| Keywords (nice-to-have) | "family owned", "three shifts", "union", "plant", "yard", "dispatch" | Signals shift culture; boosts score in refinement |

## Contact (person) filters — priority order

1. **Owner / President / GM** (companies 25–150) — at this size the owner IS the buyer.
2. **HR Manager / HR Director / People Ops** (150–1,000) — owns the engagement budget
   ($100–500/employee/yr team-building spend is their line item).
3. **Office Manager / Operations Manager** — the likely *commissioner*; champion motion.

Per account: max 2 contacts (1 buyer + 1 champion). More = duplicate-outreach risk.

## Selecting the 50 kit accounts (Rings 1–2)

Score every account in refinement (schema col I). Then:
- **Ring 1 (top 20, richer mini kit — 10-sheet pad + handwritten note):** score 5–6 only, HQ
  address verified, ideally footballSignal=1 and a named Owner/GM with a direct phone.
- **Ring 2 (next 30, mini kit):** score 4+, address verified.
- A kit account needs THREE verified fields the email list doesn't: **street address**
  (HQ, not a PO box), **contact's direct or main-line phone**, and the right **recipient
  name on the label** (the buyer, not "HR Department").
- Kit accounts are EXCLUDED from the cold sequence (schema col K) — they get the
  kit-recipient email variant, timed after delivery + first call.

## Exports & hygiene

- Export with: name, title, company, headcount, industry, LinkedIn URL, company domain, city,
  **company street address, company phone** (new in v2 — kits and calls need them).
- Apollo emails are ~70–85% accurate — do NOT send raw. Everything passes verification in
  the refinement schema first.
- Ring 3 volume target: fill whatever the depth check supports up to ~1,500 contacts.
  Don't over-export; credits don't refund.
