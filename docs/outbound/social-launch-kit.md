## Posting plan (recap — keep it ≤2 hrs/wk)
- **One content unit → five surfaces.** Each week: one ~15–30s vertical video (Kling/Seedance) +

- **Pillars (rotate):** (1) Doris-universe skits, (2) proof/product (kit unboxings, paper sheets,
