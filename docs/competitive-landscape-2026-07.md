## Verdict: the wedge is unoccupied

**No active US product combines OPL's three axes: employer-paid flat rate + strictly no-money
play + non-desk modalities (SMS / phone / paper).** Several have one or two. **Nobody has the
modality axis at all** — zero commercial SMS pick'em (one dead hobby Weebly site), zero
phone/IVR anywhere in the category, paper only as free printables with manual scoring. OPL's
paper-photo→transcription→auto-standings loop appears literally unique. The Slack/Teams
marketplace has no real NFL pick'em app (empty channel).

## Most likely entrant (capability × motive)

1. **US employee-comms platform copying Sociabble for NFL** (Staffbase/Workvivo/Firstup) —
   high capability, medium motive; structurally will never do SMS/phone/paper or point-SKU
   pricing. Plausible 2026–27 announcement.
2. **Gridiron-class host adding a "For Business" tier** — fastest possible copy of the
   *pricing*, not the modalities or compliance packaging.
3. **Pick 'Em Sports pivoting employer-paid** — mission-aligned but app-only DNA, 12–24 months
   from mattering.
4. **Splash: unlikely** — real-money handle dwarfs HR SaaS; entering employer sales would
   spotlight their gambling association.

## Implications (operative)

- **Positioning weapon:** "The category's biggest player pays commissioners a cut of gambling
  handle; we invoice the company and no employee ever touches money." The real sales
  competitor is the volunteer-run free pool — beat it on admin time, non-app inclusion, and
  HR legal safety (multiple HR advisories warn against buy-in pools).
- **Pricing:** no published employer-paid comps exist in US NFL pick'em → **OPL is
  price-setting, not price-taking.** Anchor to engagement/wellness spend (see
  CANONICAL_FACTS pricing card, repriced 2026-07-18), never to commissioner fees ($1–3/entry).
- **Moat = the modality stack** (SMS/phone/paper) — operationally unglamorous, app-first
  competitors are structurally averse. Therefore **Telnyx brand verification is the single
  most schedule-critical item**: SMS is the differentiator nobody else has and it's the gated
  one. Ship before a comms platform announces an NFL predictor module.
- **Watchlist (quarterly):** FantasyBoss (customers? integrations shipped?), Pick 'Em Sports
  (NFL launch, model pivot), Sociabble/Staffbase/Workvivo release notes (NFL module),
  Gridiron Games (business tier), thebigtourney.com (manual status check).
