Buyers decide on two things. Only one product wins on both.

Every company already does some version of this — an office pool, an engagement app, a team-building event, or a spreadsheet someone runs by hand. But a buyer really decides on just two things: will everyone actually take part, and is it cheap and low-risk to adopt. Every alternative wins on at most one. Office Pick'em League is the only one in the top-right — high participation and an easy yes — at a price that's a rounding error and a commitment of a single season.
